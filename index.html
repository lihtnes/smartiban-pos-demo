    Four product decisions behind this design

    Where it runs

    On the Android payment terminal, next to card

    No new hardware for the store, one device for every payment type, and the cashier already knows it. The till software sends the amount the same way it does for a card.

    One vIBAN per basket

    Disposable vIBAN for every checkout

    Matching by amount and time on a shared lane IBAN breaks when two shoppers pay similar sums. Fresh vIBANs come from a small pre-created pool, so creation time stays out of the queue. Each is closed after one use, and late or duplicate transfers are rejected instantly. Cost per vIBAN is tracked from day one.

    What confirms payment

    Only smartIBAN's signed status, on the terminal's own connection

    A shopper's "sent" screen proves nothing and can be faked. A store firewall blocks inbound webhooks, so the terminal opens its own secure connection and smartIBAN pushes the status down it. No merchant server. If a push is lost, the terminal checks the status every 3 seconds.

    QR now, Request-to-Pay next

    Dynamic EPC QR on the terminal screen

    Works with many banking apps today. Never a printed sticker, which fraudsters can cover. SEPA Request-to-Pay removes the scan once banks join the scheme.

    What I would measure from day one

    • Time to pay: QR shown → paid. The number that decides adoption.
    • Checkout completion: paid ÷ QR shown
    • VoP match rate: share of payers who see "name matches"
    • Auto-match rate: payments reconciled with no human
    • Exceptions per 1,000: late, duplicate, partial
    • Contribution margin per basket vs card
    • Provider health: vIBAN creation latency and error rate
    • Refund time: request → money back

    Risks I would test first

    • Speed vs a card tap. Opening a banking app is slower than tapping. Pilot with shoppers who opt in, such as at self-checkout.
    • Banking-app QR support differs by bank and country. Test the top 10 French apps before launch.
    • No chargeback. Shoppers lose card-style protection; fast refunds and clear receipts have to build that trust instead.
    • Instant limits set by banks can block large baskets. Fall back to card in one tap.
